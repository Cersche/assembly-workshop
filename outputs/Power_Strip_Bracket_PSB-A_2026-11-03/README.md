Hardware: 6 M3 x 3 x 5 inserts (2 plate, 4 cradle), 6 M3 countersunk screws (8 long assumed), wood screws or anchors for the wall (4.6 holes, 9.4 countersink, 5 mm wide wall head room).

The hangers carry no ID because they are too small. The other parts have PSB-A engraved.

**From Brian:** outlets 50 apart; keyhole 9.6 middle hole, 5 wide top and bottom, 18 total; brick 76.5 x 155 x 24.

**From the listing photo:** strip 254 x 114.3 x 27.94, keyholes 6.38 in apart.

**Assumed, not measured:** lip behind the keyhole 2, room behind the lip at least 3.5, rubber feet under 2, keyholes level with the middle of the strip, outlets centred across the strip, a spool on all 10 outlets of the two outer rows, wall type.

Hanging: slip the strip on with the hanger caps through the middle holes, then let it drop 6.7 so the necks rest at the top of the upper slots. The spools are placed to leave 8 of lift above and below the strip.

Build: `python src/build.py`. Needs OpenSCAD. `stl/dummy_*` and `stl/view_*` are viewer-only and must not be printed.
