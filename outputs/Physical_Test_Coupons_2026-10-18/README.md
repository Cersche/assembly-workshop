# Test coupons — 2026-10-18 (replaces the big 7-piece kit)

At Brian's direction: only the keystone and the snap joint get tested. These are small slices of the real parts, made with the same template the app uses. The Pi 4 print already proved the slots and screw joint.

| File | Size (mm) | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| `1_Keystone_and_corner_holes__faceplate.stl` | 48 × 4 × 42 | Right-hand end of a 1U faceplate. (1) A keystone jack snaps in from the front and holds; it should latch on the 2 mm wall left by the rear pocket. (2) The **2 rack holes on this end** (top and bottom; a full 1U plate has 4) line up with the rack post holes. |
| `2_Snap_joint__faceplate.stl` + `2_Snap_joint__tray.stl` | 24 × 20 × 42 and 24 × 30 × 3 | One snap arm and the matching tray corner. Slide the tray onto the arm: does the barb click up into the window? Does it hold, and can it be released without snapping? |

Print the faceplate pieces front-face down. Everything else (fit, strength, how long it holds) remains UNKNOWN until you try it; results apply to your printer and material only.

The old 7-piece kit (which also had the outdated 2-hole 1U plates) is set aside in `outputs/SUPERSEDED_Physical_Test_Kit_2026-10-18_old_2hole/`. Don't print from it.
