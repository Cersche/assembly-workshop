## Goal
New `device_mount` value `"wall"` (UI label: "Thin wall around it (boxes)"; short label in Place on tray: "Wall"). The device sits directly on the tray floor (no standoff, like `straps`), and the tray gets a thin wall around the device footprint, open on the cable side(s).

Geometry (device-local frame, so it follows `dev_rot` the same way holes/feet do via `dp`):
- Inner clearance `wall_clear = 0.5` mm all round the footprint; thickness `wall_t = 2`; height `wall_h = min(12, dev_h - 2)` above the floor top. Fillet not required.
- New key `wall_open`: list of open sides from `front`, `rear`, `left`, `right` (device-local; front = the device's front edge, the orange edge in the preview). Default `["rear"]`. At least one side must stay closed; at least one must be open ("leave cable access").
- An open side keeps a 10 mm return at each corner (so the corners still locate the device); everything between the returns is open. If a side is shorter than 30 mm, it is fully open.
- A closed side gets a 20 mm wide finger notch, centred, cut down to 4 mm above the floor, only on `left` and `right` (to lift the device out). Skip the notch if the side is shorter than 40 mm.
- The wall must not hit the front joint (floor block / upright / rails at `joint_x`, Y 0..11.3) or the faceplate: add a SCAD + Python + JS check with a plain message ("The wall would hit the faceplate joint; set the device back or move it sideways."), the same way `flat` checks its front gap.

Two-device trays: `wall` must work for device 1 AND device 2 (device 2's module is generated by renaming device-1 features, `equipment.py` ~1100-1180; check `dev2_mount` and `dev2_wall_open` get through). Pair reach: `PAIR_REACH['wall'] = 2.5` (`equipment.py` ~987) and its mirror `REACH` in `tray-pair.js`. Mixing `wall` with `inserts`/`clips`/etc. on one tray is allowed (both sit on the same floor height rule as `straps`). `wall` + `flat` follows the same rule as straps does today.

Everything that treats `straps` as "sits on the floor, no standoff" must treat `wall` the same: grep `straps` in `equipment.py`, `port_openings.py`, `port-openings.js`, `clearance.js`, `cable-ports.js`, `tray-placement.js`, `equipment-wizard.js`, `tray-pair.js`, and the device ghost lines in `equipment_tray.scad`. Add `wall` to `MOUNTS` (`equipment.py` line 27) and the two JS `MOUNTS` maps. Place on tray: when Wall is chosen, show checkboxes "Open sides (cables): Front / Rear / Left / Right" and the note "Device sits on the floor inside a 2 mm wall, open where you tick. Fit and retention UNKNOWN." The preview draws the wall.

Legacy safety (most important): generated SCAD sources for every spec WITHOUT `device_mount="wall"` must stay byte-identical.
