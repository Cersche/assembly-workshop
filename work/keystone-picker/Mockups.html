Keystone picker — pick one (A, B or C)

Sketches only, example: 1U plate, tray 104 mm wide centred, keystones at −100 and −75. Rules come from the existing checks: within ±102 mm, 21 mm apart, collar clear of the tray (|X| ≥ tray half + 10.4), not over a device opening. Fit stays UNKNOWN.

A — Slot strip (fixed spots)

Front view of the plate with a row of fixed snap spots every 21 mm. Green = free (click to add), filled blue = keystone (click to remove), grey hatched = blocked (hover says why, e.g. "Tray collar"). Typed X box stays below.

B — Free rail (drag / click anywhere)

Same front view, but the whole allowed band is a green rail; blocked stretches are grey with labels. Hover shows a ghost keystone at the mouse X (snaps to 1 mm, shows the number); click drops it. Click a keystone to remove. Most flexible, any X.

C — Spot chips + small preview

Small read-only front view on top, and under it a row of buttons for every allowed spot ("−100", "−79", "+79", "+100"…). Blocked ones are greyed buttons with the reason on hover. Simplest, works well on a touch screen.

My pick: B — it shows the real allowed band, lets any X, and still snaps; the typed box covers exact values.
