Checks and assumptions

  • Inside width 208 = width member length (cubes sit on the post line). Frame width 248; faceplates 252 overhang 2 mm a side.
  • Mounting depth: rail 184 + two posts 40 + two strips 16 = 240. A different strip thickness changes the rail length: rail = 240 − 40 − 2 × strip.
  • Height: cube 20 + post 400 + cube 20 = 440. 9U is 400.05; the 0.05 mm is not worth cutting for.
  • Hole line ±118.2625 falls on the post face (104–124), 5.74 mm from its outer edge.
  • Assumed, not yet checked: corner cubes are exactly 20 mm and flush with the profile; the cube screw thread (the listing says M6, 2020 ends usually take M5); strip thickness 8 mm.
  • Order tolerance ±0.2 mm, square ends. Fit, stiffness and strength UNKNOWN until built.
